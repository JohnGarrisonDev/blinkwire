# Changelog

Newest first. Written for players.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-26)

Plays better on phones:
- Full screen on every phone, iPhone included: tap "Play full screen" under the game. Tiles get bigger (a 9-wide board goes from about 27 to 35 pixels on a small phone), and the board stays clear of the notch and the exit button.
- The tile under your finger lights up with a bold ring while you press, so you can see which one is about to turn. Slide off it to cancel.
- Bigger menu and tool buttons, larger labels under the tools and the move counter.
- A little buzz when a tile turns, when a bolted tile refuses, on long-press pin and when the board lights up (Android, when sound is on).

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)

Tell us what you think.

- After the daily board, tell us how hard it was with one tap, and see how your moves compare with everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new **Tell us** card under the game: a thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes (too hard, too easy, broke on my phone...). No account, no typing.
- A Privacy link in the footer explains exactly what is counted.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release.

- **Journey:** 60 hand-ordered levels in 6 chapters, from a 3x3 warm-up to a 9x11 finale. Up to 3 stars per level.
- **Daily:** one board per day, the same for everyone, with a streak, your last seven days, and a "Copy result" button for bragging without spoilers.
- **Free play:** any size from 4x4 to 12x12 (9 wide on phones), with optional wrap edges and bolted tiles.
- Bolted tiles (already right, cannot turn) and wrap boards (wires leave one edge and come back on the other) arrive in later chapters.
- Pin tiles you are sure about, undo as much as you like, and ask for a hint when stuck (a hint caps the level at 2 stars).
- Plays with touch, mouse or keyboard. Your progress, including a half-finished board in each mode, is saved in your browser.
