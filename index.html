Blinkwire

Turn the tiles. Wake every sleepy eye in Oddvale.

How to play

  • The lamps of Oddvale are sleepy little eyes. Wire every one back to the Big Eye and they blink awake.
  • Tap a tile to turn it. Power flows out from the Big Eye along wires that join up. Lit wires glow and carry moving dots.
  • A board is solved when every tile is lit and no wire end is left loose. There are never any loops.
  • Finish at or near par for 3 stars. A hint shows a tile that needs turning, but caps the level at 2 stars.
  • Bolted tiles are already right. On wrap boards, a wire leaving one edge comes back in on the opposite side.
  • Sure about a tile? Pin it so a stray tap cannot move it.

Controls

  • Touch: tap to turn, two-finger tap to turn back, long-press to pin. The Turn button flips the tap direction; Undo, Hint, Pin and Restart are buttons under the board. Slide your finger off a tile to cancel a tap.
  • On a phone: tap Play full screen under the game for bigger tiles.
  • Mouse: click to turn, right-click or Shift-click to turn back, long-press to pin.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD move, Space turns, Shift+Space turns back.
  • Z undo · H hint · P pin · R restart · Esc menu
